**What is BOOKDE?**
BOOKDE is a brand specializing in providing hotel, homestay, and resort website design solutions with a modern interface, featuring full functionality from online booking and payment to room management.

With the motto “Easy booking – Professional management – Revenue growth,” BOOKDE is not only a website design service but also a companion for hotels in their digital transformation journey.

**Key Features of BOOKDE**
🌐 **OTA-Standard Interface – Optimized User Experience**
Modern design, compatible with computers, mobile phones, and tablets.
The interface resembles OTAs like Agoda and Booking.com, making it easy for users to get familiar with.
Easily customize logos and colors according to brand identity.

📅 **Online Booking & Room Management**
Users can select dates, view available rooms, make bookings, and receive email confirmations.
Manage room availability in real-time to avoid booking conflicts.
Supports managing multiple room types, quantities, and flexible pricing.

💳**Integrated Online Payment**
Supports payments via bank cards, e-wallets, QR codes, etc.
Safe, secure, and convenient for both customers and hotel owners.

🔄 **Connect with Major OTAs (Optional)**
BOOKDE supports API integration with platforms like Booking.com, Agoda, Traveloka, etc., through third-party Channel Managers.
Synchronize room availability and prices between the website and OTAs, optimizing room sales channels.

Manage revenue, occupancy rates, booking sources, etc.
Helps hotels make accurate business decisions.
